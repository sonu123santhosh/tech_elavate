# Chemistry Experiment Database: Image Sourcing & Visual Specifications

## 1. Visual Strategy Overview
This document specifies the image acquisition guidelines, visual criteria, search strategies, and accessibility requirements for all 15 experiments in the **AI-Powered Smart Lab Equipment Assistant** database.

### Core Visual Principles
1. **Apparatus Context over Isolated Glassware**: Images should depict the complete, assembled experimental setup in an authentic laboratory environment, rather than isolated standalone tools, so students can visualize how equipment components interface together.
2. **Definitive Experimental Moments**: Where appropriate, capture the critical diagnostic moment of the experiment (e.g., sharp pink endpoint in acid-base titration, sky-blue complex in EDTA titration, developed chromatogram with pencil solvent front, or crystal growth on a watch glass).
3. **Safety Model Compliance**: All images showing humans or hands must model exemplary laboratory safety: students/technicians must be wearing approved safety goggles, laboratory coats, and chemical-resistant gloves. Images showing bare hands handling corrosive glassware or open-toe shoes must be rejected.
4. **License Integrity**: Prioritize CC0 (Public Domain), CC-BY (Attribution), and CC-BY-SA (ShareAlike) licenses from Wikimedia Commons, Openverse, and university open educational resources (OER). Commercial, copyrighted, or watermarked stock photography is strictly prohibited.
